# Open-world dungeons research

## WoW (play, plus Staats *(S6)*)
John Staats built 90% of vanilla WoW's **non-instanced** dungeons: caves, mines, crypts. They're the zone's
mid-story set pieces, not instances:
- A **mine or cave** off the road (a starter zone's kobold mine): one entrance, a loop or a fork, 3–5 chambers,
  the zone's quest targets inside, a named mob at the deepest point.
- **Crypts and ruins** under a landmark: stairs down, side rooms, a boss chamber.
- **Outdoor elite areas**: a fortified camp or keep where every mob is elite, built for a group *(S8)*.
- They're **shared**: anyone can walk in, mobs respawn, and you meet other players there.

## Wynncraft caves *(S10)*
- A "cave" is any area with a **well-defined passage to an end goal**. Not always underground: towers, rock spires,
  whole islands and docks count.
- **Types:** normal (fairly linear), complex (several rooms, several levels), boss cave (a big arena), tower
  (a vertical climb), island.
- Each has a **suggested level**, a **length** (from about 20 seconds to 15 minutes) and a **difficulty**.
- **Loot chests** inside, the better tiers deeper, and an **exit teleporter** back to the entrance at the end.
- A **first-clear reward** (XP, money, sometimes a unique item).

## Wynncraft loot chests *(S11)*
- Four tiers, I–IV. **The harder a chest is to reach, the higher its tier.**
- Low tiers: money, common gear, unidentified gear, potions, materials. Tiers III–IV add accessories and
  exclusive items.
- A chest left alone longer gives better loot (a reason to explore off the beaten path).

## Hypixel's mining layer *(S12)*
- One big cave split into **themed quadrants** around a central nucleus. Each quadrant has its own loot and
  dangers, and the centre ties them together (bring one crystal from each quadrant). That's reference image 2's

## Rules for the Reach
1. **One open-world dungeon per zone**, set under or inside the zone's story landmark: mine, crypt, cave complex,
   tower or sea grotto, with a named boss at the end (2–8 minutes long).
2. **Shared, not instanced.** It's a combat zone with dense spawn points, linked camps, an elite tier inside and
   a boss that respawns every 10–20 minutes. Anyone in the area takes part.
3. A **tier III chest at the boss** and a **tier II chest hidden off the main route**; a one-time first-clear
   reward per player.
4. **A way back:** a lift, rope or portal stone at the end so nobody walks back through cleared halls.
5. **Loot chests on the open map in four tiers**, hidden by difficulty: tier I by roads, II off them, III at
   high or hidden spots, IV at the hardest-to-reach spots in a zone (the underside, mountain tops, behind falls).
   Per-player loot that refills on a timer.
