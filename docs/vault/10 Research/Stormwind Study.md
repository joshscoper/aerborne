# What a capital's approach does (studied from Stormwind)

The user named Stormwind as the reference for Highcrown's arrival. We take its design ideas only: no names, no
art, no layouts copied (see [[Decisions]]).

## Ideas worth taking
- **A processional approach.** The road to the gate crosses a bridge lined with giant statues of the city's heroes,
  in pairs facing across the road. You walk *between* the founders before you reach the gate, which sells the
  city's history before any NPC speaks.
- **Scale that dwarfs the player.** The statues are several times human height, on pedestals as tall as a house,
  and the gate towers rise far above them. It feels important because you're small.
- **Repetition with variety.** Every statue shares a pedestal, scale and stone, but each hero has their own pose
  and weapon, so the row reads as one composition of individuals.
- **The gate as a frame.** The road aims straight at the gate's arch, and the city shows through it. Banners hang
  in the city's colours on every face.
- **Purpose in every district.** Trade, temple, keep, harbour and crafts each have their own quarter and landmark.
- **Lettered wayfinding.** Shops hang signs naming what they sell, and signposts point the ways at junctions.

## How Highcrown uses them
- **The Walk of Wardens:** six founding Wardens in marble and granite stand on bastions down the Kingsbridge, in
  pairs facing the road: Maelis the Lanternbearer, Corwen the First Admiral, Tobren Ironhand, Hesta of
  Goldenreach, Ysolde of the Gale and Varn the Lorekeeper. They are invented here. Each is about eighteen blocks
  tall on a lettered pedestal.
- **The Wardens' Arch** at the bridge's north end names the city, with green banners on both faces.
- **Signs:** every shop, hall and inn hangs a lettered sign, and signposts at the plaza, the stairs, the market
  and the dock square name the ways.
- **The statue system** (`ReachStatues`): a figure sculpted from posed body parts at any scale, with seven poses
  and three materials (marble, granite, bronze). The docks' Aldren uses it too.
