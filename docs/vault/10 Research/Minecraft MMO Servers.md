# Minecraft MMO servers

## Wynncraft *(S13, S10, S11)*
- About 4,400 × 5,500 blocks, two continents divided by an ocean with islands; several large islands are
  provinces of their own. RuneScape was the early inspiration.
- Built by a team under a head builder: a project manager is given a project, the head builder explains the
  design, and builders claim tasks. A creative lead owns lore and the map's overall design.
- **What the province map (ref 3) teaches:** every town and wild area shows a recommended level; levels climb
  away from the starting town; a river is the spine; a corrupted zone visibly bleeds into healthy land; icons
  show quests, NPCs, caves and dungeons.
- **Caves and loot chests:** see [[Open-World Dungeons Research]].
- **Transport:** walking and horses, plus paid travel between big towns.

## Hypixel SkyBlock *(S12, refs 1–2)*
- **Islands as zones.** A hub island plus themed islands, each a strong colour and silhouette: red mesa, black
  crystal, jungle, mushroom, snow city. You can name the island from one glance.
- **Layers.** Ref 2's diagram is a mining area split into quadrants around a nucleus, with Y levels marked for
  entrance, deep and sky layers. Plan by height as well as by area.

## What Aerborne takes
1. Wynncraft's **level-labelled map** and travel by foot with paid skiffs between discovered hubs.
2. Hypixel's **strong per-island identity** and layering: the continent's top, its underside and the isles.
3. Both servers' tiered loot chests and caves, folded into our loot and zone systems.
4. Our own names, lore and builds throughout.
