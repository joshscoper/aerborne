# Open-world dungeons

Shared, non-instanced, under or inside each zone's story landmark ([[Open-World Dungeons Research#Rules for the Reach]]).
Each one is a combat zone of its own (dense spawn points, elite tier), with camps inside, a boss that respawns, a
tier III chest at the boss, a hidden tier II chest, a one-time first-clear reward and a way back out.

## The Old Granary Cellars
> Millbrook's great granary burned in the Sundering and was never rebuilt. Its cellars ran under the whole hill.
> The Choir found them first.

- **Entrance:** a collapsed stair inside the burned granary tower on the hill (the Black Granary), lit by a Choir
  brazier, so you see the glow from the road at night.
- **Layout (one loop, four rooms):**
  1. **The Grain Stair**: a broad stair down past split sacks, 2 Choir initiates.
  2. **The Long Cellar**: a vaulted hall of brick arches, rotten grain and barrels, two camps of initiates with a
     cantor each. A side passage behind fallen barrels leads to a **tier II chest**.
  3. **The Hatchery**: a round chamber with ember-coals glowing in the floor between straw mounds, hatched
     scarecrows waking as you cross. The Sower's journal lies here (quest interact).
  4. **The Threshing Floor**: a high hall under the granary's foundations, light falling through a crack in the
     ceiling (TrixyBlox's sky crack) onto the **Scarecrow King** on a throne of sheaves. Tier III chest behind him.
- **Way out:** a grain chute and rope up to the hill's far side, lowered once the King falls.
- **Respawns:** trash 3 min, the King 12 min. First clear: a Scarecrow King's Crown (cosmetic hat item is off the
  table, so a trinket) plus adventurer XP.

## Pattern for every dungeon
1. Entrance under the story landmark, lit so it's seen from the road.
2. 3–5 rooms, one loop or a fork, never a maze.
3. Camps of linked mobs, one caster each.
4. A hidden side room with a tier II chest.
5. A boss room with walls and a hero (never a paved circle), light from above.
6. A way out from the boss room.
