## Standing rules carried over from earlier work
- Expeditions use WoW for feel only: own names, lore and builds ([[Sources]] lists what was read).
- Builds are judged against the user's reference art side by side, shape first, before anyone says they look
  good. Every hero landmark must stand in open air. Every boss room needs walls and a hero, never a paved circle.
  See [[Builders and Terraformers]].
- No `run` or `runtime` package or folder names (the root `.gitignore` hides them).
- Changing a shipped world pack needs a `version` bump.
