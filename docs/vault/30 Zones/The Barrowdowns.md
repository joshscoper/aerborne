# The Barrowdowns (6–11)

> Rolling moor of heather and grey grass, lines of standing stones, grassy barrows with stone doors, and lanterns
> on poles along every path. The Downsfolk keep their dead close, and lately the dead won't keep.

- **Palette:** purple heather (allium, lilac, purple carpets on moss), grey-green grass, mossy cobble and andesite
  stones, spruce and dark oak frames, lantern posts; low mist at dawn.
- **Hero landmark:** the **Huntsman's Barrow**, a huge mound crowned with a stone ring, the Hollow Barrow's door
  in its side. Seen from the Hollow Road on arrival.
- **Hub:** **Lantern Hill**, a chapel village on a rise, lanterns on every roof. Sexton Pell lives here.
  **Outpost:** Stonewatch, a ruined watchtower over the ford to Thornwood.
- **Subzones:** Hollow Road end (6–7) · Lantern Hill (safe) · the Long Barrows (7–9) · Ledmoor (a sunken mire with
  crypts, 8–10) · the Stone Rings (8–10) · Gravepickers' Dig (9–11) · Stonewatch Ford (10–11).
- **Families:** moor wolves and ravens; the **Gravepickers** (grave-robbers with shovels and lanterns, camps in
  dug-open barrows); the **risen** (barrow thralls, crypt archers, from the Barrow expedition's roster); Choir
  diggers.
- **Main chain:** Pell → lantern-lighting along the downs → why the Gravepickers dig → Ledmoor Crypts → the Choir's
  hymnal → **The Hollow Barrow** (expedition). Side: a shepherd's lost flock, a stonemason's broken ring,
  a widow's lantern.
- **Open-world dungeon:** Ledmoor Crypts (9–10), boss the Gravewarden.
- **Rares:** Grey Mother (wolf), Lantern-Eater (a thing in the mire), Digger Mott (Gravepicker boss with a golden
  shovel).
- **Island link:** heather and the Downs' grey wool; a lantern for your island.
