# Highcrown (the capital)

> The city at the end of the world: a promontory of grey stone and dark timber jutting out over the cloud sea, its
> docks hanging off the lip and its great gate facing the Vale.

## Role
- Where islanders arrive (sky docks) and where every service lives (bank, forge, enchanter, identifier, market,
  wardrobe, jobs, parties, graves, relics, expeditions, contracts). The 31 service NPCs already have Highcrown
  backstories, so they move here as they are.
- Start of the main story ([[Main Story#Prologue Above the Clouds (your island → Highcrown)]]) and of First Flight.
- Safe zone. No combat, trading allowed anywhere inside.

## Shape (south to north, one axis)
The arrival walk is one straight line, so a new player can't get lost: **dock → stair → market avenue → plaza →
Kingsgate**, with the keep on its crag to the east, always in view.

## Terraces (as built, 2026-11-04)
After the "meh, not wow" review, the city was rebuilt to climb its hill the way Shovel's cities do (see
[[Shovel Study]] and [[Build Audit 2026-11-04]]). The ground is cut into three terraces, each six blocks above the
last. Their edges are arcs round a point by the keep, so the city wraps its crag.

| Terrace | Street y | What's on it | Roofs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Crown Ward** | 120 | Kingsbridge, Kingsgate, plaza, Hall of Callings, the **Lantern Fane** (the temple, with the tallest spire in the city), steward's houses, the Fane garden overlooking the market, the citadel steps up to the keep (132) | slate |
| **Market Ward** | 114 | **Market square** with stall rows, the **Market Hall** (an open arcade with a guild room and bell cupola) and the market cross. Guild Street to the east and Crafters' Row (smithies, weavers, alchemists, carpenters, tanners) to the west. Two ring streets with radial alleys and a terrace walk along the edge | brown, some red |
| **Dock Quarter** | 108 | Dock square, warehouses with hoists backing onto the Market Ward's wall, a dock street, cranes, the Tethered Gull, the piers and airships | red and dark |

- **Grand stairs** carry the avenue down each step. Narrower side stairs drop from the terrace walk to the docks
  east and west.
- **Terrace walls** are faced in weathered stone, with buttresses below and parapets and lamps along the top.
- **A ring wall** with towers closes the east from the north wall to the lip.

## The capital pass (as built, 2026-11-04, second review)
The user's second review asked for a city that proves it is the capital, economically, militarily, diplomatically
and culturally. This pass answers it:
- **Military:** Highcrown Keep is now an octagonal citadel on an enlarged crag:
  - a curtain wall with machicolations and eight round towers;
  - a twin-towered gatehouse with a portcullis at the head of the citadel steps;
  - the **Crown Keep** (throne hall, empty crown, chandeliers, banners);
  - barracks, stables, an armory and a training yard with butts and dummies.

  The walls form one circuit: the north wall runs into the castle's towers, the ring wall has postern arches where
  streets pass, a rim wall with towers runs along the gorge, and a parapet follows the lip.
- **Diplomatic:** the **Hall of Accord** in the castle, where envoys sit under the banners of the Reach's peoples.
- **Economic:** the treasury holds the banker, and the Market Hall the broker. Guild Street shops are furnished
  and signed, each with its keeper behind the counter. Warehouses and a quayside line the docks, three sky-galleons
  sit at the docks (one coming in), and the piers stand on trusses anchored in the cliff.
- **Cultural:** the Lantern Fane and its churchyard, the Archive, the Wayfarers' Hall on the plaza, the waystone
  arch, a copper statue of Aldren the First Skyfarer in the dock square, and the plaza fountain.
- **People where they belong:** every service NPC is placed in their venue (`highcrown.npc_<id>`), and a test
  fails if any NPC has nowhere to stand.
- **Craft:** every plan is settled like hand-placed blocks (corner stairs, joined fences, walls and panes). Every
  house is furnished, chimneys hide their fires, and street corners are rounded.

## Look
- **Palette:** stone bricks, andesite and tuff walls, deepslate footings; dark oak and spruce frames; steep **spired
  spruce roofs** with dark grey slate accents; green banners (ref 4's green emblem). See [[Build Style Guide#Highcrown]].
- **Hero:** the Kingsgate, seen from the whole avenue. Secondary heroes: the keep on its crag; the dock towers from
  the piers.
- Stalls: wool awnings in four colours only (red, white, blue, purple) with barrels, crates and composters.

## Population
Every existing service NPC, plus Captain Wren, the skiff master, an innkeeper, 6 patrolling Watch guards (the pack
already supports patrol routes), 8 townsfolk with gossip that points at the Vale.
