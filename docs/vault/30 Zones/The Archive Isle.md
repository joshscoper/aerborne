# The Archive Isle (22–26)

> The Wardens' sky archive floats off the east coast, pale stone and levitating ruins, reached by a chain bridge
> half fallen into the cloud. Its vaults were opened from the inside.

- **Palette:** white and pale quartz, calcite and diorite, gold trims, cherry and azalea in courtyards; hanging rock
  with strata below; chains everywhere.
- **Hero landmark:** **the Seal Spire**, a tall pale tower with three rings of floating stone, one per Warden. The
  Hall of the Three Wardens' door is at its foot.
- **Hub:** **Vell's Camp** in the ruins of the outer cloister.
- **Subzones:** the Wardens' Bridge (22) · Outer Cloister (23–24) · the Floating Stacks (24–25) · the Seal Spire
  (25–26).
- **Families:** sky drakes (phantom-bodied), **echoes** (Warden echoes from the Hall's roster), animated archive
  guardians (iron-golem bodies), Choir thieves.
- **Main chain:** Vell → the opened vaults → the stolen ledgers → recover three seal-keys in the Floating Stacks →
  **the Hall of the Three Wardens** (expedition) → Last Light Bastion.
- **Open-world content:** the Floating Stacks are a jumping/climbing area with tier IV chests at the top, rather
  than a dungeon.
- **Island link:** archive tomes (enchanting), calcite and quartz sets.
