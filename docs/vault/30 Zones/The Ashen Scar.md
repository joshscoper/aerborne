# The Ashen Scar (25–30)

> The wound in the middle of the Reach. A crater of ash and black glass ringed by the Brimwall's cliffs, and at its
> heart the rift, where the land never closed and the old world's fire still shows. The smoke rises from it day
> and night.

- **Palette:** ash (light grey and grey concrete powder, tuff), basalt columns, blackstone, magma and black glass
  (tinted glass, obsidian), dead trees, ember particles; red-orange glow from below at night.
- **Hero landmark:** **the Rift and the Ember Gate**, a chasm ~120 wide going right through the continent, chains
  hanging into it, and on its lip the Ember Gate (Cinder Keep's door). Its smoke column is the Reach's landmark,
  seen from every zone.
- **Hub:** **Last Light Bastion**, a Frontier Warden fortress in the Emberpass, its beacon on the Brimwall.
- **Subzones:** Emberpass (25) · Last Light Bastion (safe) · the Ashfall Flats (26–27) · the Choir's war camp
  (27–28) · the Glass Fields (28–29) · the Rift's lip (29–30).
- **Families:** ash hounds, magma creepers; the **Ember Choir** at full strength (initiates, cantors, zealots,
  brands); fire elementals (blaze-bodied); elite areas round the war camp.
- **Main chain:** Maren and Cato Flint → hold the rim → break the war camp → the Choir's Undercroft → light the
  beacon → **Cinder Keep** (raid) → epilogue "The Smoke Thins".
- **Open-world dungeon:** the Choir's Undercroft (28–29), the cult's temple dug under the war camp; boss the
  Choirmaster.
- **World boss:** the Ashen Colossus walks the Ashfall Flats every two hours (group content).
- **Rares:** Cinderback, Brand-Sister Oriel, the Glassborn.
