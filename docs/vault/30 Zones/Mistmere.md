# Mistmere (14–19)

> A great lake held in the north of the Reach, spilling off the edge as the Long Fall. On its shore, Saltreach: a
> crowd of terracotta roofs packed onto a headland, ships at every quay. Up the valley the aqueduct strides east on
> its arches. Lately the lake is warm, and the lower valley is under water.

- **Palette:** Saltreach in orange terracotta roofs, white and smooth-sandstone walls, white roads (ref 6); the
  harbour's tall timber towers (ref 5); the aqueduct in pale stone with copper sluices; mangrove marsh at the
  drowned end.
- **Hero landmark:** **the Great Aqueduct**, a line of tall arches marching east to the Ironspine. Its valley end
  sinks into the flood: the Sunken Aqueduct's door.
- **Hub:** **Saltreach**, the port city (a second, smaller city to Highcrown). **Outpost:** Cobb's Weir, the
  engineers' camp on the dam works.
- **Subzones:** Rootgate shore (14–15) · Saltreach (safe) · the Reedbanks (15–16) · the Drowned Fields (16–17) ·
  Smugglers' Cove (17–18) · Cobb's Weir (safe) · Aqueduct Road (18–19) · the Long Fall.
- **Families:** lake crabs and herons; **smugglers** (crews, a quartermaster); the **drowned** (Tidewarden's
  servants); Choir hauliers.
- **Main chain:** Cobb → fish floating → the warm stone → follow the smugglers → Smugglers' Grotto → the ledger →
  **the Sunken Aqueduct** (expedition) → Aqueduct Road to Ironspine.
- **Open-world dungeon:** Smugglers' Grotto (17–18), a sea-cave under the cliffs with a hidden dock; boss Captain
  Sallow.
- **Rares:** Old Whiskers (a giant pike), the Drowned Bride, Bosun Kett.
- **Island link:** lake fish for cooking, pearls, a terracotta roof kit.
