# Ironspine Highlands (18–23)

> The Reach's spine of mountains: grey peaks with snow on top, quarries terraced into their flanks, headframes and
> cranes on every ridge. Cragholm clings to the cliffs in tiers. Down in the dark, the gloomsteel goes on forever.

- **Palette:** the quarry recipe (tuff → cobble → andesite → light grey concrete powder), deepslate in cracks, spruce
  props and trestles, green-roofed shacks, lanterns; snow and packed ice above y 170.
- **Hero landmark:** **the Deep Foreman's Headframe**, a giant black timber hoist over a shaft on the highest
  shoulder. The Gloomforge Mines' door is in its pit.
- **Hub:** **Cragholm**, a mining town stacked up a cliff with lifts between tiers. **Outpost:** Lanternpeak, a
  mountain refuge.
- **Subzones:** Aqueduct Road end (18–19) · Cragholm (safe) · the Terrace Quarries (19–20) · Gloom Gulch (20–21) ·
  the Old Dig (21–22) · the Headframe (22–23) · Brimstone Stair (23).
- **Families:** mountain rams and goats, rock lurkers; **claim-jumpers** (rival crews); the **deepfolk** (things
  that came up the shaft); Choir overseers.
- **Main chain:** Hesk → the missing night shift → the Foreman's contract → the Old Dig → old gold with the Wardens'
  seal → **Gloomforge Mines** (expedition) → Wren flies you to the Archive Isle.
- **Open-world dungeon:** the Old Dig (21–22), a cave complex in three quadrants round a central crystal cavern
  (ref 2's diagram); boss the Gloom Matriarch.
- **Rares:** Granitehorn, the Lamp Thief, Rustjaw.
- **Island link:** gloomsteel ore, quarry stone sets.
