# Thornwood (10–15)

> Old-growth forest so tall the light comes down green. The rangers' lodges sit on platforms in the boughs. At the
> forest's heart stands the Heart Tree, and its leaves are falling in summer.

- **Palette:** giant custom spruce and dark oak (3–5 wide trunks, buttress roots), moss, ferns, podzol and rooted
  dirt, glow berries; ranger architecture in spruce and mangrove with green wool awnings.
- **Hero landmark:** **the Heart Tree**, a colossal tree on a hill, its crown above the canopy, black thorn climbing
  it. Thornwood Canopy's door is at its roots.
- **Hub:** **Bough Hollow**, a ranger lodge built round and up a giant trunk (ref 7's tall trees, taken further).
  **Outpost:** Mossbridge, a covered bridge over a gorge.
- **Subzones:** Stonewatch edge (10–11) · Bough Hollow (safe) · Fernmere glades (11–12) · Weaver's Hollow (12–14) ·
  the Thornbreaks (13–14) · Heart Tree hill (14–15) · Rootgate (15).
- **Families:** wolves, giant spiders (Mire Weavers), **thornfolk** (corrupted wood-spirits: zombies in leaf and
  thorn), Choir rot-tenders, the Rotbark Acolytes from the Canopy roster.
- **Main chain:** Ilsa → the rot in the sap → collect blackthorn → Weaver's Hollow → Verrin's last camp → the
  springs are warm → **Thornwood Canopy** (expedition) → north to Mistmere.
- **Open-world dungeon:** Weaver's Hollow (13–14), a spider-cave complex under roots; boss the Brood Matron's
  daughter, Silkmother Ysse.
- **Rares:** Old Bark (a walking stump), the White Hart (passive; its antler is a trophy), Needlefang.
- **Island link:** heartwood saplings and glow-berry cuttings.
