# Goldenreach Vale (1–7)

> Gold to the horizon: terraced wheat stepping down every slope, windmills on the ridges, and the Goldwater winding
> through it all to fall off the edge of the world at Highcrown. Lately the scarecrows walk.

## Identity
- **Palette:** gold wheat and hay, warm oak and stripped birch frames on cobble and stone-brick plinths, red
  and white awnings, cherry and apple orchards in pink and green, coarse-dirt paths. The very tall spruce of ref 7
  stand in groves over the farms.
- **Silhouette from the air:** striped terraces and windmills. From inside: the burned **Black Granary** on its hill
  in the west, the zone's dark landmark, and the Scar's smoke beyond the northern hills.
- **History:** the Vale fed Highcrown for 900 years. Burned foundations from the Sundering lie under the fields;
  ploughs turn up old-world coins.

## Map
Kingsgate opens on the south edge. The road spine runs north to Millbrook, then west along the Goldwater to the
Hollow Road. Levels rise from the gate (1) to the Black Granary and the Hollow Road (7).

## Story chains (about 18 quests)
1. **The Quiet Fields** (main, from Reeve Hollis Marr in Millbrook): find the walking scarecrows → loot ember-coals
   from them → show the coals to the smith → scout the burned windmill → the Tollkeepers took a payment →
   break the Tollkeepers' camp, take the payment (old gold and the closed-eye mark) → the Black Granary → clear the
   **Old Granary Cellars** and kill the Scarecrow King → report to Maren on the Hollow Road (breadcrumb to Act II).
2. **Tam's Gone Walking** (Thistle Farm, side): the farmer's son followed "singing" at night. Track him to Windmill
   Ridge, find him dazed in the burned mill, carry him home. First sight of the Choir.
3. **Sweet Trouble** (Brindle Orchards, side): the cider-maker's bees swarm boars; thin the boars, collect wild
   honeycomb, deliver.
4. **Grist for the Mill** (Millbrook miller, side): gnawers in the granary yard; clear them, fetch a millstone pin
   from the Tollroad's ruined toll house.
5. **Dailies (2):** a gnawer cull in Kingsgate Fields; a scarecrow burning on the Terraces.

Level pacing: chain 1 runs the whole zone; side chains sit beside it in the same subzones (Kaplan's interleave).

## Mobs (see [[Bestiary#Goldenreach Vale]])
Wildlife: Field Gnawer, Bristle Boar, wild bees. Local people: the **Tollkeepers** (bandit, crossbow, the
Toll-Captain). Story enemy: **Ember Choir** initiates and cantors (from level 5). Oddity: **ember-hatched
scarecrows**.

**Rares:** Old Grizzlehide (boar, orchards), Hollow Jack (a scarecrow that walks Windmill Ridge at night),
Gilt-Tooth (gnawer queen in the granary yard's drains).

## Open-world dungeon
**The Old Granary Cellars** (6–7): see [[Open-World Dungeons#The Old Granary Cellars]].

## Chests (4 tiers)
Tier I ×6 along the roads; tier II ×4 off them (orchard well, behind the toll house, a terrace step, a hollow oak);
tier III ×2 (top of the tallest windmill; behind the upper cascade of the Goldwater); tier IV ×1 on a ledge under
the lip below the Hollow Road, reached by a cliff path.
