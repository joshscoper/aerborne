# Sunscar Breaks (21–25), side zone

> East of the Brimwall the land has been baking for nine hundred years in the Scar's heat. Red and orange canyons,
> mesas, dry riverbeds, and nomad caravans that know where the water is.

- **Palette:** badlands terracotta bands (the biome's own), red sand, dead bush, acacia, sandstone; nomad tents in
  white, orange and brown wool.
- **Hero landmark:** **the Sunscar Arch**, a natural arch over the main canyon.
- **Hub:** **Dustwatch**, a walled caravanserai on a mesa.
- **Role:** a parallel zone for 21–25 beside the Archive Isle. No expedition; a **world boss**, Brimfather, roams
  the Brimstone Warrens' mouth. The Wardens' Bridge to the Archive Isle leaves from its east coast.
- **Carries over** the old map's desert spirit (Sandmere's bazaar, the colosseum) with new names and builds.
- **Open-world dungeon:** Brimstone Warrens (23–24).
