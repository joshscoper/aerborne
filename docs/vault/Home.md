# Wardens' Reach design vault

The research and design vault for the new central map, which replaces the "MMORPG" map on production. It is
Aerborne's story-driven progression area. It sits between your skyblock island and the expeditions: you fly in
from your island, follow each zone's story to its dungeon door, and come home with materials your island can't
make.

Open this folder (`docs/vault`) as a vault in Obsidian. Reference images live in `_refs/`, which is local only
and never committed.
