# Factions

The three reputation factions already in `content/npcs.yml` stay; the Reach gives each a home and a reason.

| Faction | Exists? | Who they are in the Reach | Commissioner | Home |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Frontier Wardens** | yes (`frontier_wardens`) | The Reach's soldiers and road wardens, sworn to the Wardens' old oath. They hold the passes and the Scar's rim. | Cato Flint | Highcrown keep; Last Light Bastion |
| **Rift Scholars** | yes (`rift_scholars`) | Archivists who study the seals, the Raising and the rift. | Lyra Senn | Highcrown's Archive Walk; Vell's Camp |
| **Free Delvers** | yes (`free_delvers`) | Miners, salvagers, smugglers-turned-honest: anyone who goes down. | Garrick Mott | Cragholm; the Old Dig |
| **Skyfarers' Guild** | new (no reputation) | Airship crews. Run the skiffs and the sky docks, fly islanders to the Reach. Captain Wren is one. | Captain Wren | Highcrown sky docks |
| **The Crown of Highcrown** | new (no reputation) | The capital's Steward and council. | Steward Aldric | Highcrown keep |
