# Lore timeline

Builds on the lore already in the expeditions' guide books (`resources/expeditions/*.yml`, `guide.lore`).
Everything here must agree with them.

| Era | What happened | Where you see it in the Reach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Old World** | One land under one sky. The Wardens of Ember, Frost and Stone keep it. | Buried ruins under every zone; old roads that lead off the lip into nothing |
| **The Sundering** | Pyrrhax, the Ashen Sovereign, sets the old world burning. His cultists march. The Huntsman holds the hill road while the last carts climb. | The Hollow Road; the barrows; burned foundations in Goldenreach |
| **The Raising** | The Wardens bind Pyrrhax beneath Cinder Keep, then tear the survivors' lands free and lift them above the smoke. The biggest piece becomes the Reach; the tear in its middle never heals. | The Ashen Scar and its rift; the Brimwall; broken bridges hanging off the lip |
| **The Wardens' Road** | The Wardens link the islands with a road through the sky, kept by guardians like the Old Sentinel. They plant the Heart Tree to bind the islands' roots, and write the Binding into three seals in their sky archive. | Waystones and Warden statues along the roads; the Heart Tree; the Archive Isle |
| **The Long Calm** (about 900 years) | Highcrown is founded on the southern promontory. Farms in Goldenreach, rangers in Thornwood, the engineers' guild builds the aqueduct to cool the stone over Cinder Keep, miners follow gloomsteel down in Ironspine. | Every town; the aqueduct; the mines |
| **The Embers Below** (now) | Steward Ashkar, the Sovereign's servant who never stopped serving, wakes the deep fires. A cult, the **Ember Choir**, grows in secret among the desperate. The dead walk in the barrows, rot climbs the Heart Tree, the Tidewarden drowns her valley, the Deep Foreman digs straight down. Islanders arrive from their skyblock islands to help. | The whole season's story: [[Main Story]] |

## Things everyone in the Reach knows
- The smoke from the Scar is "the Sovereign's breath". It has been thin for centuries. This year it is thick.
- Anyone who falls off the lip is lost, which is why skyfarers wear lines.
- Highcrown has a Steward and no king. The last king's crown hangs empty in the keep: "until the Wardens return".
- Warden statues mark old roads. Touching one for luck is a habit, and it's where the waystones are.

## Things only the story reveals
- The Ember Choir's mark is a flame inside a closed eye. It turns up in every zone before anyone knows what it is.
- Ashkar pays in **old gold** from before the Sundering: coin nobody in the Reach can have minted.
- Each disaster is a step toward breaking the three seals and opening the Ember Gate.
