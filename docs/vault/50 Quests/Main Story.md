# Main story: The Embers Below

One chain from your island to the raid. Each act is a zone; each act's mystery leads to the next zone; each act
ends at that zone's expedition door. It follows the lore already in the expeditions' guide books, so the
dungeons become the climaxes of stories you've walked through.

Recurring characters: **Captain Wren** (Skyfarers' Guild, flies you in, turns up at each act's end with a skiff),
**Steward Aldric** (Highcrown, who sends you), and **Maren Ashdown** (a Frontier Warden scout who has followed the
Choir's trail alone for a year and meets you at every zone's last hub).

## Prologue: Above the Clouds (your island → Highcrown)
- On your island, a Guild notice: the Reach needs hands. Talk to the skiff master at your island's dock, or use
  `/reach`, and Wren's airship lands you at **Highcrown's sky docks**, under Highfall.
- Wren introduces Highcrown and sends you up the market avenue to Steward Aldric.
- Aldric: the smoke over the Scar is thick for the first time in centuries, and Goldenreach's farms have gone
  quiet. Before he sends anyone into the Vale, the Guild tests new adventurers: **First Flight** (the existing
  tutorial expedition, level 2), from Wren's dock.
- Back from First Flight with your Adventurer's Charter, Aldric sends you through the **Kingsgate**.

## Act I: The Rot in the Fields (Goldenreach Vale, 1–7)
- **Mystery:** scarecrows walk at night, gnawers swarm the granaries, farmhands go missing, and the soil is warm.
- **Trail:** the Tollkeepers (road bandits) were paid to look away while someone buried **ember-coals** in the
  fields. The payment is old gold. Every coal is wrapped in cloth marked with a flame inside a closed eye.
- **Climax:** the Old Granary Cellars under Millbrook's ruined granary, where a Choir "Sower" has been hatching
  scarecrows from ember-coals. You kill the Scarecrow King; the Sower escapes west down the Hollow Road.
- **Hand-off:** Maren Ashdown, waiting at the Hollow Road, has followed the same mark from the barrows.

## Act II: The Hill Road Remembers (Barrowdowns, 6–11)
- **Mystery:** grave-robbers dig into the barrows; the dead stand up in their old ranks; lanterns on the hill keep
  going out.
- **Trail:** Sexton Pell. The robbers are digging for the Huntsman's barrow on the Choir's orders; the Sower was
  their go-between. In Ledmoor Crypts you find the Choir's hymnal and a sprig of black thorn from Thornwood.
- **Climax:** **The Hollow Barrow** (expedition, 8): lay the Huntsman to rest.
- **Hand-off:** Pell's letter to Ranger Ilsa, who wrote about "black thorn in the Heart Tree".

## Act III: Sap and Ember (Thornwood, 10–15)
- **Mystery:** the Heart Tree's leaves are falling in summer; thornfolk walk; Verrin never came down.
- **Trail:** Ranger Ilsa. The rot is in the sap; the roots drink from springs warmed from below, and the warm water
  comes from Mistmere.
- **Climax:** **Thornwood Canopy** (expedition, 12): the Thornheart Shaman.
- **Hand-off:** Ilsa sends you north through Rootgate to the engineers at Mistmere.

## Act IV: The Drowned Valley (Mistmere, 14–19)
- **Mystery:** the lake is warm, fish float, the Tidewarden has sealed the floodgates and the lower valley is
  under water.
- **Trail:** Engineer Cobb. Smugglers have been shipping crates of "warm stone" out of the valley at night. The
  Smugglers' Grotto holds the ledger: buyer in Ironspine, paid in old gold.
- **Climax:** **The Sunken Aqueduct** (expedition, 16): the Tidewarden.
- **Hand-off:** the ledger points along the Aqueduct Road to Cragholm.

## Act V: Straight Down (Ironspine Highlands, 18–23)
- **Mystery:** the night shift never came up; the Deep Foreman hired every hand he could find and told them to dig
  straight down.
- **Trail:** Old Miner Hesk. The Old Dig shows the Foreman's shaft breaking into the Gloomforge's halls. The old
  gold bears the **Wardens' own seal**: it was taken from the Archive.
- **Climax:** **Gloomforge Mines** (expedition, 20): the Deep Foreman.
- **Hand-off:** Wren flies you across the Wardens' Bridge to the Archive Isle.
- **Side zone:** Sunscar Breaks (21–25), reached down the Brimstone Stair, for players who want more before the
  Isle: the nomads, the heat and a world boss.

## Act VI: The Seals (the Archive Isle, 22–26)
- **Mystery:** the Archive's vaults were opened from inside; the Wardens' echoes have woken.
- **Trail:** Archivist Vell. The seals hold the Binding; whoever breaks all three opens the Ember Gate. The name
  in the stolen ledgers is **Steward Ashkar**.
- **Climax:** **The Hall of the Three Wardens** (expedition, 24).
- **Hand-off:** Vell and Maren to Last Light Bastion on the Scar's rim.

## Act VII: The Ember Gate (the Ashen Scar, 25–30)
- **Mystery solved:** the Choir's war camp fills the Scar; the rift glows; the Gate is open.
- **Trail:** hold the rim with the Frontier Wardens, break the Choir's Undercroft, light the Bastion's beacon.
- **Climax:** **Cinder Keep** (raid, 26).
- **Epilogue:** after the raid's last boss falls on any tier, Aldric's last quest, "The Smoke Thins", turns the
  Scar's smoke column thin for that player (a per-player world change) and grants the season title.

## How the story uses the expeditions
- Each act's last quest is "go through the door" with objective **clear expedition X** (new objective type,
  see [[Technical Plan#New systems]]). The door is a physical expedition entrance in the zone.
- The expeditions' existing givers (Pell, Ilsa, Cobb, Hesk, Vell) stand at their zone's main hub and keep their
  dailies.
- Wren appears at each act's end beside a skiff (a second NPC anchor per act).
