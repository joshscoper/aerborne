# Practice Builds

Use a small disposable plot or an offline design exercise when learning a new technique. Each assignment isolates one decision. Give the player a task, keep a control version, and compare from the same camera. The dimensions here are practice budgets. Keep the verified lesson and its viewing conditions in [[Review Record Template]].

| Exercise | Constraint | Evidence that earns the next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gate thumbnail | Two towers, one opening and a foreground frame. Use one block type; make two versions with the same total height but different tower spacing. | From mid-street, identify the gate and see the way through. From off-axis, check which spacing keeps the arch visible. |
| Hillside workshop | Build the hall from [[Town and Building Kit]] across a slope with a four-block difference between corners. Give the public entrance and loading entrance separate uses. | Walk from road to both entrances, inspect the uphill sill and trace the downhill support to ground. Save a section through the worst corner. |
| Cliff and grove | Fit one spur, a gully, a seep and three differently sized trees in a 32 × 32 patch. Keep one reveal open from the trail. | Shots from above, below and at the turn should show where water drains and where the next ridge appears. Identify a revision that improves one view without losing another. |
| Encounter room | Build the [[Playable Spaces]] cellar without decoration, then place the actual cover and final furniture. | Repeat the same group movement before and after dressing. Remove or relocate any prop that traps a retreating player. |
| One complete zone walk | Connect an arrival, a working settlement, a hero site and a return loop. Carry one regional material through all four and change the skyline at the boundary. | A fresh player can describe the order of places, find the route back, and identify the zone from two different viewpoints. Record the first hesitation and revise that transition. |

## Transfer beyond the Reach

For a coastal industrial world, replace the farm's supply route with ore brought from a cut to a rail shed and out to a ship. Use the crane boom as the distant landmark, the shed opening as the street destination and bright paint on the loading doors as a near cue. Test the crane against open sky at the wharf and from the inland track.

For a hot, arid settlement, start with the water supply, a shaded market and a route between them. Use low adjoining roof masses to enclose the market and one taller cistern tower to guide the approach. Render a shaded arcade beside sunlit plaster. Walk from full daylight through the arcade to the well and adjust the openings until all three spaces read clearly.

In each transfer keep the player task, three distances and evidence standard. Recompute construction, climate response, materials and proportions for that world. Repeat the weakest exercise after changing biome, scale or approach direction.
