# Millbrook Terrace Study

**Brief.** Build a small farm approach in [[Goldenreach Vale]] where a player can carry a sack from the road to a receiving door. From the bend, wheat should fill the near view, the door should identify the destination, and spruce crowns should rise behind the roof. The source is ref 7 in [[Reading a Build Reference]]. This is a proposed teaching plot with local coordinates. Check the actual site, routes and reservations before assigning world coordinates.

## Plan the trip

![[millbrook-study.svg]]

Allow 31 × 25 blocks. Local X increases east, Z increases south, Y below means the **top elevation of a walking surface**. Take the arrival at the southern boundary looking north. Stake these inclusive footprints:

| Part | X | Z | Surface or role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival lane | 2–6 | 11–24 | Start at Y 0. Keep five usable blocks through the first view. |
| Lower wheat bench | 8–29 | 16–23 | Y 0. Break its near edge around the bend, with a narrow strip of exposed earth. |
| Middle wheat bench | 8–29 | 11–15 | Y +1. Leave a worker gate beside the cart lane. Reserve water and service access for the crop beds. |
| Upper yard | 2–23 | 8–10 | Y +2. Extend the landing across to the receiving door at X 16. |
| Farm hall | 12–20 | 1–7 | 9 × 7 shell. South-facing entrance at X 16, flush to the upper yard. Load the roof ridge east–west so its long eave shades the receiving door. |
| Tree group | 24–30 | 1–8 | Reserve one tall spruce and one smaller crown. Prune the near side if foliage crosses the door sightline. |

**North–south section along the lane at X 4.** Hold Y 0 from Z 24 through 16. Rise one stair north at Z 15, continue at +1 through Z 12, rise again at Z 11, then land at +2 from Z 10 through 8. Turn east on this flat yard and then north into the hall. Use stairs across the full lane width, with the high sides uphill. Face the exposed terrace ends and return the stone into earth at both ends. The generous middle landing gives the player time to see the yard before the turn.

**Building section.** Put a stone sill under timber posts at the yard level. Carry a beam above the receiving door, recess the plaster and give the roof an overhang. Fit the sack bench on the east wall inside, leaving the entry and turn clear. Plant a broad gold field in front and reserve the red-and-white awning for the door. On the first pass use only three materials for ground, shell and foliage so the route can be assessed without colour cues.

## Inspect three views

| Stand here | Look for | First revision if it fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X 4, Z 22, player height, looking toward the yard | A wheat edge in the foreground, receiving door above it, trees framing the far right | Widen the break in the field edge or move a competing crown. Recheck before raising the building. |
| X 4, Z 10, looking east across the landing | The yard and a recognizable turn to the receiving door | Pull stored sacks inside the work bay and expose the door's near jamb. |
| Doorway, looking back down the slope | Two distinct terrace lips and the route to town | Lower the nearest hedge at the landing and align its gap with the lane. |

**What would count as success.** A player starting at the lane can reach the receiving bench and return without jumping, trampling crops or using a quest marker. In a small image the fields, roof and trees form three distinct depth layers. Compare the reference and the approach in ordinary lighting. Until that walk and comparison happen, this example is a design hypothesis.

**Transfer exercise.** Turn the same supply chain into a Mistmere fishing quay. Replace the stepped crop beds with waterline, landing deck and raised store. Retain the three depths and receiving-door test. Recompute every elevation from the quay and choose the region's palette. This tests whether the method travels beyond the farm.
