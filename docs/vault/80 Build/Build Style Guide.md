# Build style guide

The checklist every build passes before anyone calls it done, and the palette for each zone. Sources:
[[Builders and Terraformers]], [[Reference Images]].

## Checklist (judges' faults, adapted)
- [ ] **Massing first.** The shape works in plain blocks before any texture. No "box with detail on".
- [ ] **One axis to one hero.** The approach aims at the landmark; the sight line stays clear.
- [ ] **Hero in open air** and about 25% bigger than feels right.
- [ ] **No run longer than 7 blocks straight**: walls step, roofs break, terrain curves.
- [ ] **Depth in every wall**: pillars one proud, windows one back, stone footing under timber.
- [ ] **Smoothing everywhere or nowhere**: stairs and slabs on every big form (terrain, roofs, rock).
- [ ] **One light source** for shading; dark recesses, lit edges.
- [ ] **Value ramps, not splatter**: neighbouring tones only, clusters, darkest at the foot.
- [ ] **Small next to big** for scale: barrels, carts, fences, signs by every tall thing.
- [ ] **Nothing in a line**: props placed in groups, at angles.
- [ ] **Middle height filled**: lamps, signs, awnings, trees between ground and roofline.
- [ ] **Focal points finished most**, not least.
- [ ] **Light leads**: hubs and exits lit, dead ends dark.
- [ ] **Walkable**: two abreast and three high on every route; nothing floats.
- [ ] **Makes sense in the world**: why is this here, who built it, how do they get water.
- [ ] **Compared with the reference image side by side**, shape first.

## Terrain
- Grass never runs to a cliff edge or a riverbank: coarse dirt, gravel and rooted dirt there.
- Cliffs overhang a little; their faces use stone → andesite → tuff ramps, darker low down.
- The underside is half the island: strata bands (stone, andesite, tuff, deepslate, a clay or terracotta seam),
  hanging drips, roots and vines near the lip.
- Every waterfall foams (bubble columns are not needed; use white stained glass and snow layers sparingly at the
  foot of a fall, or nothing).

## Palettes
### Highcrown
Walls: stone bricks, cracked and mossy in cluster ramps, andesite footing, tuff accents. Frames: dark oak and
spruce. Roofs: steep spruce stairs with deepslate tile ridges, spires capped with dark oak fences and lanterns.
Banners: green and white (the Highcrown emblem). Docks: spruce planks, stripped spruce piles, chains, lanterns.

### Goldenreach Vale
Fields: wheat in terraces, hay bales at the edges, composters and barrels at field corners; terrace walls of
cobble and mossy cobble with stone-brick caps; coarse-dirt and path lanes. Houses: stone and cobble plinths, oak
and stripped birch frames, white terracotta or calcite infill, oak or spruce roofs, red-and-white awnings.
Windmills: stone tower, spruce cap, white wool sails. Trees: tall custom spruce (ref 7), cherry and apple
orchards (oak with flowering azalea). Ruin (Black Granary): stone bricks blackened into blackstone and basalt at
burn marks, charred spruce, campfire smoke.
