# Island visitor economy

What island visitors, the Farmstead and contests pay, and the guards against farming them. Checked 2026-11-12.

## Faucets

| Source | Pays | How often |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visitor offer | Marks = item value × 1.6 × rarity pay (×1 Uncommon to ×3.5 Mythic) × showcase bonus; island XP; Favour 5 / 12 / 30 / 75 (Special 40) | One arrival per 20 min (down to 11 min fully upgraded), up to 7 waiting, filled while away |
| Visitor bonus item | Expedition raw material, or an XP Booster (Mythic 50%, Special 30%) | Rare 8%, Legendary 30%, Mythic 100%, Special 50% |
| Visitor milestones | 100 to 3,000 Favour each, 10 total (about 10,200 Favour) | Once per island |
| Friendship | 50 / 150 / 400 Favour, a Name Tag at Friend, a rare item or booster at Old Friend | Once per visitor per island |
| Crop milestones | 50 × tier Favour (tiers I-VI) | Once per crop per player |
| Farming contests | Bronze / silver / gold medals per crop | Every 2 h through the Double XP Weekend |

## Sinks

- Visitor's Stall: bench 1,200; Swift Arrivals 300 / 900 / 2,000; lure 60; Name Tag 40; Compost ×8 80; raw materials ×4 200; XP Booster 3,000 Favour.
- Asking a visitor for something else: 20 × (rarity + 1) Favour.
- Gear nicknames: a Name Tag plus 200 Marks (plain) or 750 Marks (gradient).
- Every accepted offer consumes the items asked for.

## Guards

- Rerolling an offer keeps its bonus: only the request changes, so rerolls can't fish for boosters (an XP Booster costs 3,000 Favour at the stall).
- Lures are limited to three a day per island, so Favour can't be spent fishing for Mythic visitors.
- The same visitor never waits twice at once; a full island doesn't bank arrivals.
- Crop milestones and contests count only ripe, naturally grown crops on an island the harvester belongs to (the resource ledger rejects placed blocks).
- Bonus items that don't exist on the server pay 150 Marks instead; stall and medal goods that don't exist are refused or refunded.

## Watch

- Mythic offers at island level 40+ can pay a few thousand Marks. That's fine while they stay about 2% of arrivals; revisit if level-40 islands become common.
- If Favour piles up unspent, add cosmetic stall goods rather than raising prices.
