# NerdSMP map commission brief

## Deliverables

- Overworld: 8,000 × 8,000 blocks.
- Nether: 4,000 × 4,000 blocks.
- End: 6,000 × 6,000 blocks.
- Editable source projects plus exported Paper-ready worlds.
- Coordinate/region manifest in CSV or JSON.
- Asset and contributor provenance statement.
- Commercial, perpetual modification and hosting rights for NerdSMP.

The maps are fully reachable within normal survival progression. They are seasonal, resource-bearing worlds rather than decorative hubs.

## Required geography

- Safe onboarding spawn and a neutral central market.
- Four regional market sites with player-stall plots.
- Connected road, river, portal, and landmark navigation language.
- Twelve reusable rift/event sockets with protected staging buffers.
- At least twelve delves/dungeons across the dimensions, built as resettable subregions.
- Three finale arenas with safe spectator, reset, and staff-control positions.
- Renewable extraction sites visibly integrated into world lore.
- Claimable settlement land separated from protected roads and POIs.

## Technical constraints

- No illegal blocks, unsupported block states, command blocks, embedded plugins, or unexplained datapacks.
- Portals must respect the intended dimensional scale and never place players outside borders.
- Spawn and travel routes must be passable without elytra or paid services.
- Supply pristine schematics or region copies for each resettable dungeon/extraction site.
- Identify every container, spawner, marker entity, boss anchor, event trigger, no-claim buffer, and reset boundary.
- Test with Java and Bedrock collision, visibility, swimming, crawling, and interaction behavior.

## Acceptance

The commission is accepted only after automated region scans and manual QA find no escape routes, void traps, inaccessible resources, broken portals, copyrighted imports, unauthorized assets, destructive command blocks, or soft locks. Final payment should follow delivery of source files and the signed provenance/licensing statement.
