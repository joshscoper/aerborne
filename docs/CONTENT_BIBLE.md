# NerdSMP content bible

## Promise

NerdSMP is an economy-first social survival server with an arcane-frontier RPG layer. Players should remember who supplied an expedition, who found a relic, and what the community changed—not merely how many levels they ground.

## Pillars

1. **Useful specialization:** everyone progresses, but two masteries create dependable reasons to trade.
2. **A world worth protecting:** commissioned spaces are playable geography, not a lobby surrounding disposable terrain.
3. **Readable danger:** custom enemies telegraph abilities and use familiar hitboxes on Java and Bedrock.
4. **Shared consequences:** asynchronous campaign contributions and scheduled finales both matter.
5. **Trustworthy commerce:** purchases are visible cosmetics, never concealed power.

## Progression vocabulary

- Skills: Mining, Forestry, Farming, Fishing, Hunting, Excavation, Smithing, Alchemy.
- Level cap: 50.
- Mastery eligibility: level 25.
- Seasonal mastery limit: two.
- Respec: 10,000 earned Marks and a 14-day cooldown.
- Gear: six initial three-piece relic collections; tradeable, provenance-tagged sidegrades.

The initial collection themes are Veilwalker, Emberforged, Verdant Oath, Tideglass, Hollowfang, and Starfall. Raw damage bonuses must remain modest; set effects should primarily alter utility, resource decisions, positioning, or encounter responses.

## Campaign template

Each 10–14-day campaign contains:

1. a visible world disturbance and briefing;
2. gathering, crafting, hunting, and exploration contribution lanes;
3. per-account daily contribution caps;
4. server-wide milestones that alter an event site or unlock a service;
5. two finale performances within 24 hours;
6. one reward claim per account, regardless of finale window;
7. a permanent season chronicle entry.

Failure may alter story presentation or the cost of a public project, but must not permanently cripple late joiners.

## Parity-first mobs

Reserve selected vanilla entity silhouettes for themed variants, then distinguish them with equipment item models, texture changes, sounds, particles, names, and telegraphed behavior. Boss mechanics must work with ordinary movement, attack, interact, sneak, and inventory controls. Community extensions for custom Bedrock entities are not launch dependencies.

## Content review checklist

- Gives at least one mastery a market role without making it mandatory.
- Has an earned acquisition route and no store dependency.
- Has a Java/Bedrock parity proof.
- Has rollback/admin controls.
- Has an explicit currency source or sink classification.
- Has anti-farm and duplication cases.
- Uses licensed original assets and all-ages presentation.
