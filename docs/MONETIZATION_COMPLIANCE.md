# Monetization and trust policy

This operational policy implements a deliberately conservative reading of the current Minecraft Usage Guidelines. It is not legal advice and must be reviewed before substantial revenue or paid staffing.

## Approved catalog

- Supporter: USD $6.99 monthly or $69.99 annually.
- Cosmetic badges/emotes: approximately $2.99–$4.99.
- Cosmetic pets, auras, and item appearances: approximately $4.99–$8.99.
- Cosmetic theme bundles: approximately $12.99–$19.99.

Supporter may provide only a badge, title/name palette, emotes, cosmetic wardrobe presets, and a visual keepsake. Direct purchases and paid keepsakes are account-bound, non-tradeable, and persist across declared wipes.

## Forbidden catalog

Never sell Marks, items with functional stats, XP, mastery access, job multipliers, claims, market capacity, homes, travel convenience, queue priority, moderation treatment, server votes, keys, crates, randomized rewards, capes, cape-like visuals, or access to gameplay areas.

No paid currency is needed. Earned Marks cannot be bought, sold, cashed out, transferred to other servers, or represented as Mojang/Microsoft currency.

## Store requirements

- Tebex is merchant of record and supplies payment/purchase history.
- Product contents, duration, pricing, renewal, cancellation, refund handling, and seasonal persistence appear before checkout.
- Fulfillment, expiry, refund, and chargeback commands include the Tebex transaction ID and are idempotent.
- Unknown identities enter a support queue; they never receive a substitute gameplay reward.
- A monitored email—not only Discord—is published for ownership, privacy, and purchase support.
- The privacy policy describes UUID/XUID, IP, chat/moderation records, gameplay telemetry, and transaction-reference handling.

## Required disclaimer

> NOT AN OFFICIAL MINECRAFT SERVER. NOT APPROVED BY OR ASSOCIATED WITH MOJANG OR MICROSOFT

Display it on the website, store, server listing, campaign marketing, and influencer briefs. Sponsored creators must disclose the relationship under applicable advertising rules.

## Review cadence

Re-check the official Usage Guidelines quarterly and before adding a product category, advertising arrangement, virtual currency, new server address, or paid entitlement. Record the review date, reviewer, policy URL, and resulting catalog decision.
