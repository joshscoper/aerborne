# Ten-week whitelisted beta

## Cohorts

- Week 1: ten players.
- Week 3: ten additional players.
- Week 5: final ten players.

Every applicant receives the wipe disclosure, store persistence statement, rules, privacy notice, Java/Bedrock pack requirements, and feedback schedule before accepting a whitelist place.

## Launch gate

- Java and Bedrock complete onboarding, linking, claims, skills, relic use, market trade, graves, PvP opt-in, and campaign contribution.
- Purchase, duplicate, renewal, expiry, refund, chargeback, offline-known-player, and unknown-player cases pass.
- A 30-player scripted finale stays at 20 TPS with p95 MSPT below 40 ms.
- Backup restore completes inside four hours.
- No open critical duplication, item loss, authentication, entitlement, or permission-escalation defect.
- Store, website, email contact, prices, history, terms, privacy notice, and disclaimer are public.

## Wipe contract

Reset worlds, Marks, skills, masteries, jobs, claims, gear, stalls, listings, graves, and campaign state. Preserve canonical account links, moderation records, purchase history, paid cosmetics, and explicitly announced beta legacy cosmetics.

## Measurement

Record onboarding completion, D1/D7/D30 return behavior, distinct trading partners, market volume, money supply and sinks, mastery distribution, campaign participation, grave recovery, support load, Java/Bedrock completion gaps, performance, and monetization-fairness survey results.

The beta passes when onboarding is at least 80%, D7 retention 40%, D30 retention 25%, weekly trade participation 60%, weekly campaign participation 50%, critical Java/Bedrock completion differs by at most ten percentage points, and monetization fairness reaches 80% positive responses.
