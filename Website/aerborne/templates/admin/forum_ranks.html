Ranks

Ranks are the coloured tags shown beside names on the forum and profiles. Give them to members under Forum → Members. Each rank picks an avatar frame, shown around the member's avatar on posts and profiles; members without a rank get the Member frame. Ranks can also let members moderate or post announcements.
